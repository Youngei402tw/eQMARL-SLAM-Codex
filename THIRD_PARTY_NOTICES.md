# Reference provenance and notices

The Stage 00 scaffold contains original planning text and a small standard-library validator. No upstream source tree, checkpoint, dataset, figure, or paper text is vendored.

The reference implementation is Alexander DeRieux and Walid Saad's [news-vt/eqmarl](https://github.com/news-vt/eqmarl), pinned in `references.json`. At the inspected commit, `LICENSE.md` and `pyproject.toml` specify **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)**. It is not an MIT-licensed repository.

Before adapting or redistributing reference code, retain its applicable attribution and license notices, identify changes, and maintain a file-level provenance record. Inspect the corresponding notices in each user fork and dependency before copying its files. This planning scaffold does not relicense upstream material or select a blanket license for future code.

The source paper is [eQMARL: Entangled Quantum Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning for Distributed Cooperation over Quantum Channels](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.17486). Cite the paper and reference implementation in research reports. Preserve the distinction between reference reproduction, implementation corrections, and new research contributions.
